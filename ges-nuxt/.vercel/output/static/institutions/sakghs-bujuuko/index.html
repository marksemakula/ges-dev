St. Andrew Kaggwa Gombe High School – Bujuuko

St. Andrew Kaggwa Gombe High School – Bujuuko is a secondary school in the Gombe Education Service network, nurturing tomorrow's leaders through quality education, character formation and academic excellence.

As a member of the Gombe Education Service network, St. Andrew Kaggwa Gombe High School – Bujuuko shares the GES vision of academic excellence, character formation and global readiness. Admissions, term dates and school news are published on the school's own website.
